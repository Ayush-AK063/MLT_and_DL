# Topic 6: What Can AI Do? (Quiz — Capability Check)

Source: Lecture 2, slides 40–41 ("What Can AI Do?").

---

## 1) Technical Explanation

The lecture presents a diagnostic quiz — a list of real-world tasks — asking which are achievable by present-day AI systems. This exercise is meant to calibrate students' intuitions against the actual (uneven) state of the art, since AI capability is highly **task-specific**, not general. The lecture's answer key (✓ = yes, ✗ = no, ? = uncertain/partial) is:

| Task | Status |
|---|---|
| Play a decent game of Jeopardy | ✓ Yes |
| Win against any human at chess | ✓ Yes |
| Win against the best humans at Go | ✓ Yes |
| Play a decent game of tennis | ✓ Yes |
| Grab a particular cup and put it on a shelf | ✓ Yes |
| Unload **any** dishwasher in **any** home | ✗ No |
| Drive safely along the highway | ? Uncertain/partial |
| Drive safely along University Avenue (complex urban street) | ✗ No |
| Buy a week's groceries on the web | ✓ Yes |
| Buy a week's groceries at a physical store (QFC) | ✗ No |
| Discover and prove a new mathematical theorem | ? Uncertain/partial |
| Perform a surgical operation | ✗ No |
| Unload a **known** dishwasher in collaboration with a person | ✗ No |
| Translate spoken Chinese into spoken English in real time | ✓ Yes |
| Write an intentionally funny story | ✓ Yes (partially/inconsistently, per the lecture's icon) |

**Pattern to notice**: AI tends to excel at tasks that are well-defined, digital/virtual, or take place in **structured, controlled environments** (games with fixed rules like chess/Go, web transactions, translation). It tends to struggle with tasks requiring **general-purpose physical manipulation in unstructured, unpredictable real-world environments** (any home's dishwasher, complex urban driving, surgery, physical collaboration with humans) — this gap is sometimes called **Moravec's Paradox**: tasks that are hard for humans (advanced logic, chess) are often easy for AI, while tasks trivial for humans (grasping an unfamiliar object, walking through clutter) remain hard for AI.

Note: this lecture material reflects the state of AI capabilities at the time the slides were authored; some answers (e.g., highway driving, dishwasher unloading) have shifted over time as robotics and autonomous-vehicle technology has advanced, which is itself an illustration of how fast task-specific AI capability can change.

## 2) Simple English Explanation

This is a fun quiz slide meant to test your intuition: "Which of these things can AI actually do right now?" It turns out AI is really good at some kinds of tasks and really bad at others — and the pattern is interesting.

**AI is generally good at:**
- Structured games with clear rules (chess, Go, Jeopardy)
- Digital tasks (shopping on a website, translating speech)
- Well-defined physical tasks in controlled settings (picking up one specific object)

**AI generally struggles with:**
- Tasks that need it to work in **any** random, messy, real-world environment (unloading a dishwasher in a home it's never seen before)
- Complex real-world physical situations with lots of unpredictability (driving on a busy, complicated street; performing surgery)
- Tasks requiring true creativity or discovery, which are hit-or-miss (proving a brand-new math theorem, writing something reliably funny)

**The big lesson**: AI isn't "smart" or "dumb" in one single way — it's extremely capable at some very specific kinds of problems, and still far behind humans at others, especially anything involving flexible physical action in an unpredictable world. This is sometimes surprising, because it flips our usual assumption — the things that seem "hard" for humans (like chess) are often easy for AI, while things that seem "easy" for humans (like tidying a random kitchen) are often still hard for AI.

## 3) Hinglish Explanation (Beginner-Friendly)

Ye slide ek **maza-maza quiz** hai jisme pucha gaya hai: "In kaamon mein se AI abhi kya kar sakta hai?" Answer interesting hai — AI kuch cheezon mein bahut achha hai, aur kuch cheezon mein abhi bhi weak hai.

**AI abhi ye kar sakta hai (✓):**
- Chess mein kisi bhi insaan ko haraana
- Go game mein best players ko bhi haraana
- Jeopardy jaisa quiz game khelna
- Ek specific cup uthana aur shelf pe rakhna
- Internet pe grocery shopping karna
- Chinese bolne ko real-time English mein translate karna
- (Kaafi hadd tak) ek funny story likhna

**AI abhi ye nahi kar pata theek se (✗):**
- **Kisi bhi ghar ka** dishwasher unload karna (matlab general/random real-world kaam)
- Ek complicated, busy road (jaise "University Avenue") pe safely drive karna
- Physical store mein jaakar khud grocery kharidna
- Surgery karna
- Kisi insaan ke saath milkar dishwasher unload karna (collaboration)

**Uncertain/adhoora (?):**
- Highway pe safely drive karna (kuch hadd tak ho raha hai, lekin fully reliable nahi)
- Naya mathematical theorem discover aur prove karna

**Sabse important observation (pattern):**
AI un kaamon mein bahut achha hai jinke **rules fix hai aur environment controlled hai** (jaise chess, online shopping, translation). Lekin AI un kaamon mein weak hai jinme **real duniya ka messy, unpredictable environment** ho — jaise randomly kisi bhi ghar ka dishwasher unload karna, ya busy road pe drive karna.

Ye ek interesting cheez hai jise **Moravec's Paradox** kehte hai: jo kaam insaano ke liye **mushkil** lagta hai (jaise chess khelna) wo AI ke liye **aasan** hai, lekin jo kaam insaano ke liye **bilkul aasan** lagta hai (jaise ek nayi jagah pe haath se object uthana, ya ghar saaf karna) wo AI ke liye abhi bhi **mushkil** hai.

**Note**: Ye answers us waqt ke hisaab se hai jab lecture banaya gaya tha — technology tezi se badalti hai, isliye kuch answers (jaise highway driving) aane wale saalon mein change ho sakte hai.

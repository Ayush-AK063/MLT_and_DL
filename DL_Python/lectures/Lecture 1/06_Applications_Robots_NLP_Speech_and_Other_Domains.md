# Topic 6: Applications II — Drones and Robots, Natural Language, Speech, and Other Domains

Source: Lecture 1, slides 29–31 (Drones & Robots; Natural Language Processing; Speech processing; Other possible applications).

---

## 1) Technical Explanation

- **Drones & robots (slide 29):** managing the movement of a robot or drone (pictures: quadcopters; a humanoid robot at a sink).
- **Natural Language Processing (slide 30):** recommender systems, sentiment analysis, question answering, information extraction from websites, automated e-mail reply. The slide's figure shows **word embeddings**: words as points in a vector space where directions carry meaning — *man→woman* and *king→queen* are parallel arrows (gender), *walking→walked* and *swimming→swam* are parallel (verb tense), and *country→capital* pairs (Spain–Madrid, Italy–Rome, Germany–Berlin, Turkey–Ankara, Russia–Moscow, Canada–Ottawa, Japan–Tokyo, Vietnam–Hanoi, China–Beijing) form parallel lines. This is a concrete example of a *learned representation* — see [[01_Traditional_vs_End_to_End_Learning_and_Hand_Designed_Features]] in Lecture 2. (The slide shows the figure without naming it; the name "word embeddings" and the *man–woman / king–queen* reading are standard background.)
- **Speech processing (slide 31):** speech-to-text, and generating a particular voice for a given text (text-to-speech).
- **Other possible applications (slide 32):** language translation, weather prediction, genomics, drug discovery, particle physics, surveillance, cryptography, generative AI "and many more".

The point of the list is breadth: the same learning machinery applies to images, text, sound and scientific data. (Surveillance and cryptography are listed neutrally on the slide; surveillance in particular carries well-known privacy concerns that the slide does not discuss.)

---

## 2) Simple English Explanation

More places where deep learning is used:

- **Robots and drones:** deciding how to move.
- **Language:** suggesting what you might like (recommendations), judging whether a review is happy or angry, answering questions, pulling facts from web pages, and replying to e-mails. The slide's little 3-D charts show words placed so that similar relationships point the same way: "man is to woman as king is to queen".
- **Speech:** turning your voice into text, and turning text into a spoken voice.
- **Everywhere else:** translation, weather forecasts, DNA research, designing new medicines, physics experiments, security cameras, codes and secret messages, and AI that creates new content.

## 3) Hinglish Explanation (Beginner-Friendly)

Deep learning aur kahan-kahan use hota hai:

- **Robots aur drones:** kaise move karna hai ye decide karna.
- **Language:** recommend karna ki aapko kya pasand aayega, review khush hai ya gusse mein ye judge karna, sawalon ke jawab dena, web pages se facts nikalna, aur e-mail ka reply likhna. Slide ke chhote 3-D charts mein words aise rakhe hain ki similar relationships ek hi direction mein jaate hain: "man se woman waise hi jaise king se queen".
- **Speech:** aapki awaaz ko text mein badalna, aur text ko bolti hui awaaz mein badalna.
- **Baaki sab jagah:** translation, mausam ki forecast, DNA research, nayi dawaiyan design karna, physics experiments, security cameras, codes aur secret messages, aur naya content banane wala AI.

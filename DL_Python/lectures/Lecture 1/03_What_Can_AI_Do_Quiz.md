# Topic 3: "What Can AI Do?" — The Quiz and Its Answers

Source: Lecture 1, slides 12–13 (What Can AI Do? — quiz, then the same slide with the answers marked ✓ / ✗ / ?).

---

## 1) Technical Explanation

The slide asks: *"Which of the following can be done at present?"* The second version marks each item. Reading the ticks directly from the slide image:

| Task | Slide's mark |
|---|---|
| Play a decent game of Jeopardy? | ✓ |
| Win against any human at chess? | ✓ |
| Win against the best humans at Go? | ✓ |
| Play a decent game of tennis? | ✓ |
| Grab a particular cup and put it on a shelf? | ✓ |
| Unload any dishwasher in any home? | ✗ |
| Drive safely along the highway? | ? |
| Drive safely along University Avenue? | ✗ |
| Buy a week's worth of groceries on the web? | ✓ |
| Buy a week's worth of groceries at Big Bazaar? | ✗ |
| Discover and prove a new mathematical theorem? | ? |
| Perform a surgical operation? | ✗ |
| Unload a *known* dishwasher in collaboration with a person? | ✗ |
| Translate spoken Chinese into spoken English in real time? | ✓ |
| Write an intentionally funny story? | ✓ (tick is drawn partly over the bullet; read as ✓) |

**Pattern the quiz teaches.** Tasks in a *closed, well-defined world* (board games, a web shop, a single known object) are solved; tasks needing *open-world robustness, dexterity and safety* (any home, any road, a physical store, surgery) are not.

**Flag — the answers are dated.** The slide gives no date or source for this quiz, so its marks should be read as "the state of AI when the slide was made", not as today's status. For example, language models now write jokes and can help with some proofs, and highway driving is partly automated, but fully autonomous surgery and a general-purpose home robot remain open problems.

## 2) Simple English Explanation

The teacher shows a list of 15 things and asks "can a machine do this today?" Then he reveals the answers.

- **Yes:** playing Jeopardy, beating every human at chess and Go, picking up a cup, shopping online, translating speech live, writing a funny story.
- **No:** emptying any dishwasher in any house, driving on a busy city street, shopping inside a real store, doing surgery, working side by side with a person to unload a dishwasher.
- **Maybe:** highway driving, discovering new maths theorems.

The lesson: machines are great in neat, rule-bound worlds, and still weak in messy real-world places. Also remember these answers are old — AI keeps moving.

## 3) Hinglish Explanation (Beginner-Friendly)

Teacher 15 cheezon ki list dikhate hain aur poochte hain "kya aaj machine ye kar sakti hai?" Phir answers dikhate hain.

- **Haan:** Jeopardy khelna, chess aur Go mein har insaan ko harana, cup uthana, online shopping, live speech translate karna, funny story likhna.
- **Nahi:** kisi bhi ghar ka dishwasher khaali karna, busy city road par gaadi chalana, asli store mein shopping, surgery, kisi insaan ke saath milkar dishwasher khaali karna.
- **Shayad:** highway par driving, naye maths theorem khojna.

Seekh ye hai: machines saaf-suthri, rules wali duniya mein kamaal hain, aur gande real-world jagahon mein abhi kamzor. Aur ye answers purane hain — AI aage badhta rehta hai.
